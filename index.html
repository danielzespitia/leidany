About the Workshop

You don't have to choose between your business and your family

We know being an entrepreneur is a passionate journey, but it can often feel lonely and overwhelming. The pressure of scaling your company while maintaining harmony at home can lead to burnout and guilt.

Synergy was born from a fundamental philosophy: true leadership does not require sacrificing your personal peace. This workshop is designed to provide you with practical tools, emotional management strategies, and a proven system to delegate effectively.

Join a community of women just like you and discover how to transform your approach to lead with purpose in your business while being fully present, with quality, for the ones you love most.

  • Conscious productivity strategies
  • Managing guilt and expectations
  • Female business support network

The Curriculum

What You Will Learn

A structured curriculum in 4 progressive modules, designed to create real and sustainable changes in your daily dynamic.

Module 1

Conscious Leadership

Redefine success. Learn to identify your core values, overcome imposter syndrome, and lead from a place of authenticity.

Module 2

Time Management

Productivity techniques adapted to real life. Time blocking, intelligent prioritization, and learning how to elegantly say "no".

Module 3

Effective Delegation

Systems to build a reliable team. Learn to let go of operational control so you can focus on strategic growth.

Module 4

Harmony at Home

Family communication tools, setting healthy boundaries, and creating routines that nurture your bond with your loved ones.

Includes work materials and follow-up

By participating in this program, you will receive an exclusive workbook, management templates, and a post-workshop group mentoring session.

Is this for you?

Who Should Attend

Synergy is not just another business course. It is an integration program for women who are ready to elevate their lives comprehensively.

Scaling Founders

You have a growing business, but you feel like the operational day-to-day is consuming all your energy and leaving no room for strategic planning.

Entrepreneur Mothers

You struggle with "mom guilt." You want to be fully present during family dinners and milestones without feeling anxious about pending emails.

Overwhelmed Leaders

You find it hard to trust others with your work. You need to learn how to delegate effectively to prevent burnout and regain your personal time.
